**Full description**

Picking someone up at the airport shouldn't mean standing at Arrivals guessing.

Arigreet turns your pickup into one link. Send it by WhatsApp, Messages or email. The person you're meeting opens it in their browser. No app, no account.

Then you both just tap:
• They tap I've landed, and you know straight away.
• You tap On my way, Running late or At Arrivals, and they see it.
• When they're ready, you both see how far apart you are, moving live on a map.
• Close by? Your phone becomes a big, bright GreetBoard with their name. They can make it flash so you spot each other across the hall.
• Can't see each other? They tap I can't find you and your board lights up.

Made for drivers, hotels, transfer companies, families and friends.

Private by design. Location is only shared during the pickup, only with the person you're meeting, and it stops the moment you meet.

Passenger pages work in English, Greek, French, Spanish, Italian, German and Portuguese.
