Arrivals is a crowd of people holding phones, all calling someone. Arigreet turns your pickup into one link. They tap when they land. You see them coming.

How it works

One link, nothing to install

Add their name and flight. Send the link on WhatsApp, Messages or email. It opens in any browser.

They tap, you know

I've landed. Collecting my bags. I'm ready. One button at a time, in their language.

A live map, then an arrow

When they're ready you both see each other moving. Close up, one big arrow points the way with the distance in metres.

Your phone becomes a name sign

The GreetBoard fills your screen with their name. They can make it flash so they find you across the hall.

Private by design

Location is shared only during the pickup, only with each other. It stops and is deleted the moment you meet.

Made for bad signal

Taps made in a dead zone wait on the phone and send the second the signal comes back.

Talk to us

Questions.

Everything people ask before their first pickup.

Does the person I'm meeting need the app?

No. They tap your link and it opens in their phone's browser. No download, no account, no password.

What if they lose signal in baggage claim?

Their taps wait on the phone and send as soon as signal comes back. Their pickup details stay saved, so they can still see where to meet you.

Who can see my location?

Only the person you're meeting, and only after they tap I'm ready. Sharing stops and the location is deleted when you tap We met.

Which airports does it work at?

7,884 airports, searchable by city, name or code, with terminals for the busiest ones.

What languages does the passenger see?

English, Greek, French, Spanish, Italian, German and Portuguese. Their page picks their phone's language, and they can switch it.

How accurate is the finding arrow?

A few metres outdoors. Inside terminals GPS is weaker, so the app says so, and the GreetBoard and the "I'm at Exit 3" button take over for the last steps.
