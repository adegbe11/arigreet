Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Do they need the app?

No. Your link opens in their phone's browser.

No signal in baggage claim?

Taps wait on the phone and send when signal comes back. The meeting point stays on screen.

Who sees my location?

Only the person you're meeting, only after they tap I'm ready. Deleted when you meet.

Which airports?

All 7,884 with an airport code, with arrival guides for 3,840.

How accurate is the arrow?

A few metres outdoors. Inside, the meeting point and the GreetBoard finish the job.
